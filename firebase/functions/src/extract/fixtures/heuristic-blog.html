Grandma's Banana Bread

No structured data here — just a family blog post.

Ingredients

  • 3 ripe bananas, mashed
  • 1/3 cup melted butter
  • 1 cup sugar
  • 1 egg
  • 1 tsp baking soda

Instructions

  1. Preheat oven to 350°F.
  2. Mix bananas and butter.
  3. Stir in sugar, egg, and baking soda.
  4. Pour into loaf pan and bake 60 minutes.
